## What is Duix Avatar?

Duix Avatar is a free and open-source AI avatar project developed by Duix.com that enables you to create ultra-realistic digital humans for video production. Unlike traditional costly 3D digital human approaches, Duix Avatar leverages AI-generated technology to create digital humans at a fraction of the cost.

Seven years of research and development have resulted in a groundbreaking method to train digital human models using real-person video data, reducing production costs from hundreds of thousands of dollars to just $1,000. Now, with this open-source release, you can create your own AI avatar and produce videos at zero cost.

## How It Works

Duix Avatar is built on three core technologies:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Voice Cloning">
    Advanced AI technology generates similar or identical voices based on voice samples, covering context, intonation, and speech patterns
  </Step>
  <Step title="Speech Recognition">
    Automatic speech recognition (ASR) converts human speech into computer-readable text format
  </Step>
  <Step title="Computer Vision">
    Video synthesis uses visual processing including facial recognition and lip movement analysis to ensure avatar lip movements match voice and text content
  </Step>
</Steps>
